Your Right

As a tenant under the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999, you have the right to reasonable notice before eviction and protection from arbitrary lockout.

What This Means

Your landlord cannot evict you without a 30-day written notice and a valid reason recognized under the Act.

What You Can Do

Send a written acknowledgment of the notice period. If challenged, approach the Rent Control Court.

Source

Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999 — Section 15
