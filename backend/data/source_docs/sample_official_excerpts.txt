Code on Wages, 2019 (sample official-style excerpt for retrieval tests)

Section 17. Time limit for payment of wages.
The employer shall pay or cause to be paid wages to the employees on the due date.

Section 19. Deduction of wages.
Deductions from wages of an employee shall be made only in accordance with this Code.

Section 45. Payment of undisbursed wages in case of death of employee.
Subject to the other provisions of this Code, all amounts payable to an employee as wages shall, if such employee dies, be paid to the person nominated by him.

NALSA legal services awareness note: workers who are not paid wages may approach labour authorities and District Legal Services Authorities for legal aid.
